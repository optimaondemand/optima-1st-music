Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“How can sounds and movements show us the difference between fast and slow?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can identify whether music or a beat is fast or slow
2
I can sing or move to show a fast tempo and a slow tempo
3
I can create a simple pattern that changes between fast and slow
🔤
Key Words
Vocabulary
⚡
FAST
Tap to reveal definition
Music or a beat that moves quickly, like a cheetah running at top speed.
🐢
SLOW
Tap to reveal definition
Music or a beat that moves gently and takes its time, like a turtle walking through the grass.
👀
Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Clap a steady beat slowly like a sleepy turtle, then speed it up to clap like a zooming cheetah. Did you notice your hands moved differently for each speed? I wonder what other things in my life are fast or slow, like animals or vehicles?

💭
After the Lesson
Reflection

What is one way you showed a fast tempo and one way you showed a slow tempo today?

🎮
Interactive Activity
Fast or Slow? Sort

Watch each thing move across the screen. Is it fast or slow? Tap the right basket to sort it!
